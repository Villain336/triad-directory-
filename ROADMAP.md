# NCSB SEO/GEO Roadmap

Non-code playbook items from the SEO/GEO Marketing Strategy. These sit outside
the Next.js app itself but materially affect organic and LLM-citation
performance. Treat this as the source of truth for the off-site workstream.

## Now (this sprint)

- [ ] Google Business Profile: claim the NCSB brand GBP, fill out categories,
      service areas, primary photo, service menu, and hours.
- [ ] Google Search Console: verify `ncservicebusinesses.com`, submit the
      sitemap, and set preferred domain.
- [ ] Bing Webmaster Tools: verify and submit sitemap.
- [ ] IndexNow: subscribe and ping on city-service publish / listing update.
- [ ] Seed `/how-we-verify` link into every city-service page prose (done in PR).
- [ ] Publish 3 cornerstone blog posts:
  - "What licenses does a plumber need in NC? (2026 guide)"
  - "The real cost of a new roof in the Triad — 2026 market index"
  - "When do you need a permit in Greensboro? A homeowner's guide"

## Next 30 days

- [ ] Wikipedia entity: create or improve the NCSB company article (must meet
      GNG — use the Crunchbase profile + press as citations).
- [ ] Wikidata: create `Q` identifier for NCSB, link to Wikipedia, GBP, LinkedIn.
- [ ] Crunchbase profile, LinkedIn Company Page, Twitter/X business account.
- [ ] HARO / Qwoted: answer 5 relevant queries per week, especially "local
      directory" and "home services" beats.
- [ ] Reddit presence: seed r/NorthCarolina, r/Greensboro, r/raleigh with
      genuine community answers citing NCSB pages where relevant (no spam).
- [ ] Cost-index press release: publish the "NC Home Services Cost Index 2026"
      as a press-release-worthy data story with regional breakdowns. Pitch to
      WFMY, WRAL, WXII, Triangle Business Journal.
- [ ] 10 link-building asks: local chambers of commerce, university career
      centers, community colleges with trades programs.

## Next 90 days

- [ ] Profound or Otterly.ai subscription to track LLM citation frequency.
- [ ] Surveys: run quarterly consumer panel (n≥300) on NC home services usage
      and publish findings with a shareable chart pack.
- [ ] Video: record a short "How NCSB verifies a plumber" explainer for YouTube
      and embed on `/how-we-verify`.
- [ ] Paid search: $2k/mo test budget targeting high-intent city-service
      queries ("emergency plumber greensboro nc"). Measure CAC vs. lead
      fulfillment.
- [ ] Paid social: Meta retargeting for cart-abandoned quote-request flow.
- [ ] Apprenticeship guides: publish a guide per city+trade aimed at the labor
      side of the marketplace (attracts backlinks from vocational programs).

## Next 180 days

- [ ] NC Licensing Board partnerships: reach out to NCBEEC, NCLBGC, and
      NCLCLB to become a listed consumer resource.
- [ ] Local paper syndication: pitch quarterly cost-index to NC papers as
      sponsored-but-factual content.
- [ ] Quarterly editorial refresh of top 70 city-service pages (manual pass on
      the 2,303 programmatic pages — target the highest-traffic 3% first).

## Not doing (yet)

- Nationwide expansion beyond NC — strategy is NC-dense coverage first.
- Marketplace transactions (payments, bookings on-site). Lead-gen only for v1.
- Mobile app. PWA is enough for the directory use case.

## Metrics we watch

- Organic sessions / month (target: 50k by month 6, 250k by month 12)
- Indexed pages in GSC (target: ≥ 2,800 within 90 days of launch)
- LLM citation share-of-voice: queries where NCSB is cited in ChatGPT /
  Perplexity for "[service] in [NC city]"
- Lead volume / month (quote-request form submissions)
- Cost per verified lead from paid channels
- % listings with current verification (target: ≥ 97% at all times)
